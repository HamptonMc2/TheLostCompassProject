# Website Purpose

The Lost Compass Project website exists to:

* Inspire exploration
* Tell meaningful stories
* Build a community
* Introduce the conservation mission
* Collect email subscribers
* Establish the brand identity

The website is NOT primarily an ecommerce store.

The website is NOT a nonprofit website.

The website is a storytelling and exploration platform that will eventually support apparel, conservation efforts, and community initiatives.

# Build Philosophy

Do not build a store.

Do not build a nonprofit website.

Do not build a corporate website.

Build a premium outdoor storytelling platform that feels established, timeless, adventurous, and inspiring.

The website should make visitors feel like they have discovered the beginning of a journey.

Every design decision should support:

Find Your Way.
Explore More.
Leave It Wild.
