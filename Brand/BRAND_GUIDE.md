# Brand Mission

The Lost Compass Project exists to inspire exploration, storytelling, conservation, and connection to the natural world.

Every adventure leaves a mark.

Our goal is to make it a positive one.

The Lost Compass Project supports The Lost Compass Conservation Project, an initiative focused on supporting the people and organizations protecting the places we love.
